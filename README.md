# Olist E-Commerce Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end E-Commerce Data Analysis project based on the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset.

The objective of this project is to clean, analyze and visualize e-commerce data to identify meaningful business insights related to sales, customers, sellers, products, payments and customer reviews.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The project focuses on answering key business questions such as:

- How are sales and orders performing?
- Which product categories generate the most sales?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- Which states have the highest customer and seller presence?
- How are customer reviews distributed?
- Which products and categories perform better?
- What insights can help support data-driven business decisions?

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The analysis identified several notable observations, including:

- **São Paulo** has the highest customer and seller presence.
- **Cama, Mesa e Banho** is among the strongest-performing product categories.
- **Credit Card** is the most commonly used payment method.
- Higher review scores, particularly **5-star reviews**, represent a large share of customer reviews.
- Customer and seller activity is highly concentrated in major states such as São Paulo.
